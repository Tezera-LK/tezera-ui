tezera freelancer · low fidelity

Low-fidelity prototype of the freelancer web portal: the core screens and how they connect, in greyscale with no styling. Use the flow map to see each journey, or click through the screens: highlighted areas are real links.

About these screens
  • Greyscale on purpose: this shows structure and flow only. Colour, type and imagery are in the high-fidelity file.
  • Lighter hotspots show a short message instead of changing screen. Download SVG saves the current screen so it can be dropped into Figma.
